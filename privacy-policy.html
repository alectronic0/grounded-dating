PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy — Grounded Dating

Last updated: 28 April 2026

1. Who We Are

Grounded Dating (“Grounded Dating”, “we”, “us”, “our”) is a UK-based company providing curated, in-person blind date introductions. Grounded Connections Ltd is the data controller for personal information provided to us.

Contact Email: info@groundeddating.com

2. What Information We Collect

When you sign up or interact with us, we may collect:

  • Full name & Date of birth
  • Email address & Phone number (WhatsApp)
  • Gender & Who you are open to meeting
  • Availability & Preferred social venue preferences
  • Communications with our matchmaking team

Identity verification prior to confirming a date is conducted securely via our third-party provider (Didit at didit.me). ID documents are handled securely and not retained longer than necessary.

3. Special Category Data

Information about who you are open to meeting may reveal sexual orientation. Under UK GDPR, this is classified as special category data. We only process special category data where strictly necessary for matchmaking and where you have given explicit consent.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Verify your identity and maintain safety standards
  • Assess suitability for curated introductions
  • Match you based on availability and venue preference
  • Communicate logistics about your blind date
  • Improve our service based on post-date feedback

We do not sell your personal data or use automated decision-making algorithms.

5. Lawful Basis for Processing

Contract: To provide the dating introduction service.
Legitimate Interests: To operate and safeguard our community.
Explicit Consent: For processing special category data (such as sexual orientation).

6. Data Sharing & Retention

We do not publicly share your personal info. Limited details are shared with your match (first name only prior to meeting) and partner venues (name for table bookings). Inactive user data may be retained for up to 6 years for legal compliance and safety. You may request data deletion at any time.

7. Your Rights under UK GDPR

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict your data, as well as lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO at ico.org.uk).
