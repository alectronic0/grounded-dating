❓ FAQ

Got Questions? We Have Answers

How does a Grounded blind date work? +
You answer our questionnaire detailing your non-negotiables, undergo ID verification via Didit, and join the waiting list. Our matchmaker sets you up on a blind date at a public London venue (coffee shop, pub, gallery, bookshop). No profiles or bios—just their name and venue plans!
How does pricing work? +
Grounded Dating is not a subscription trap. We operate on a pay-as-you-go basis depending on the social experience. Prices generally start from £20 per date introduction.
How do you verify members? +
Every client is ID-verified using third-party verification (Didit at didit.me), so everyone in our network is genuinely who they say they are.
