HOW IT WORKS

About Grounded Dating

“Making Real Life Dating Easier”

Dating apps have stopped doing the one thing they were designed to do… help people meet someone.

Grounded Dating is built for people who are done swiping but haven’t found a way to meet someone who matches their values or their intentions.

Grounded Dating is affordable matchmaking the way it used to work: one person, doing the work of actually understanding you, and introducing you to someone worth meeting. No algorithm. No infinite scroll. No profiles designed to keep you swiping instead of meeting someone.

🗺️ THE PATHWAY

How It Works

1

1. Eligibility check

Before anything else, we make sure you’re a good fit for Grounded Dating: based in London, and genuinely open and intentional about finding a relationship. This isn’t a formality. The people who do best here are the ones who are serious about it, and this is where we check that from both sides. Message us today +44 7429 398306 to schedule a call with a Grounded Matchmaker.

Message Matchmaker
2

2. ID verification & onboarding

Every client is ID-verified, so everyone in our network is genuinely who they say they are. This stage also includes your discovery meeting, the time it takes to properly understand who you are, what you’re looking for, and what’s actually worked or not worked for you before. Your first date with a match is included.

didit.me ID Verified
3

3. The search

Once onboarding is complete, you’re added to our waiting list and we start looking. We check our existing network first and headhunt beyond it when your search calls for someone new.

4

4. Introduction

When we find someone we think is right, we handle the date logistics and introduce you to your match through a blind date.

5

5. The date, and what happens after

We handle the logistics so all you have to do is show up. Afterwards, we ask for feedback because every introduction that follows is shaped by what we learn from the last one.

Why it’s priced the way it is

You pay a one-off fee to join, and after that, only when there’s someone worth introducing you to. No subscription, no ongoing charge for access you’re not using. It’s built to reward being intentional with your match, through a grounded date.
