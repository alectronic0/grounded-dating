OUR MISSION

Make Real Life Dating Easier

We are on a mission to redefine dating for the modern age in a world craving serendipity and spontaneous human connection.

Grounded Dating challenges optimisation-driven mate selection and replaces it with structured real-world experimentation. The platform does not promise compatibility; it enables discovery. Technology acts as an exit ramp to real-life interaction, not a destination.

Structural Issues Within The Dating App Market

  • Gamification of mate selection: Mechanics that misalign with relationship formation and encourage addictive scrolling.
  • Saturated with vanity filtering: Normalising the search for “perfection” with theatres of profiles over real presence.
  • Emotional burnout: Repetitive digital pre-date interactions that drain excitement before meeting.

The Grounded Dating Solution

Grounded Dating removes profile construction, swiping, and extended texting. Users verify identity via Didit, select availability and where they feel comfortable meeting someone new, and are introduced directly to a pre-arranged blind date in a public venue. The product standardises logistics while preserving spontaneity.
