Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our real-world blind date matchmaking experience in London.

What makes Grounded Dating different from traditional dating apps? +
Apps maximise choice; Grounded Dating maximises action. Apps reward aesthetics; Grounded Dating rewards showing up. Apps promise outcomes; Grounded Dating creates the conditions for spontaneous human connection. Apps prioritise algorithms; Grounded Dating enables discovery.
How does a blind date work? +
A blind date is a prearranged meeting between two people set up by a 3rd party with the aim of exploring romantic potential. You won’t see photos or bios—just their name and your date plans in person at a public venue (coffee shop, pub, gallery, bookshop). Chemistry and compatibility only emerge in person.
How does ID verification work? +
Every member undergoes secure identity verification via Didit (didit.me) to ensure all participants are real, verified individuals.
What happens if the spark isn't there? +
That’s part of the experience—not every match will be magical! But every real-life date helps you grow and clarify what you’re looking for. If it’s a no, it’s just one coffee. No hard feelings, no ghosting.
How much does it cost? +
Grounded Dating is not a subscription trap. We charge a pay-as-you-go price depending on the social experience. Prices generally start from £20 per date introduction.
Where do the dates take place? +
We work with curated local London venues—coffee shops, pubs, galleries, bookshops. You tell us where you feel comfortable meeting someone new, and we do the rest.
Is this speed dating? +
Not quite. Speed dating involves meeting several people in one night for a few minutes each. Grounded Dating sets you up on one quality blind date at a time with someone picked specifically for you by our matchmaker.
