Join Our Early Team

Building London’s first curated blind date service—a trust-first alternative to gamified dating apps.

We’ve arranged dates all through word of mouth. Every single person showed up. Our members describe the experience as unlike anything dating apps can offer. We’re now building the systems to scale—and we’re looking for early hires with real equity potential.

Vacant Roles

PR & Operations Lead

Full-Time • London

Work directly alongside founder Nadia Aghios as our first hire sitting at the intersection of operations, PR, and human matchmaking.

What You'll Do:

  • Operations: Manage the end-to-end member journey from sign-up through date confirmation.
  • Matchmaking: Read member questionnaires carefully and work with the founder to curate compatible matches.
  • Acquisition & PR: Pitch our story to journalists, podcasters, and media; build community growth strategies.
  • Retention & Feedback: Be the human face of the brand—warm, trustworthy, and responsive.

Software Developer

Engineering • London / Hybrid

Build our core member onboarding and matching platform. Clean, fast, mobile-responsive, and trustworthy.

What You'll Build:

  • Member Web App: OAuth & email auth, Stripe checkout (£10 waitlist fee), ID verification API integration (Didit / Onfido / Veriff), multi-step questionnaire, and member dashboard status tracker.
  • Admin Dashboard: Internal member matching interface, scheduling tools, member status pipeline, and reporting metrics.
  • Automated Communications: Transactional email engine with configurable templates.
  • Security & Infrastructure: GDPR-compliant ID verification handling, role-based access control, encrypted data storage.
